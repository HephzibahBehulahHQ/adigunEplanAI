# ENG-0208 — Smart-home lighting cabinet — Future expansion provision

Status: **planned; not an implemented, tested or installation-approved design**.

Domain: Residential installations. Base system: Smart-home lighting cabinet.

Engineering scope: actuator channels, wall inputs, lighting loads, bus power supply.

Distinct requirement: Provide a defined additional outgoing circuit and reserved control/terminal capacity with load allowance.

Functional acceptance: Validate reserved space, protective-device coordination assumptions and available supply capacity.

Use one native project, four section containers, and the 30 sheets below. Engineering assumptions must be explicit; add sheets when complexity requires them. Read [the handoff](../../NEXT_AGENT.md) before implementation.
