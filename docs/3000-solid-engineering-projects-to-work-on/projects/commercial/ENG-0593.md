# ENG-0593 — Car-park electrical services — Hardwired permissive chain

Status: **planned; not an implemented, tested or installation-approved design**.

Domain: Commercial buildings. Base system: Car-park electrical services.

Engineering scope: lighting groups, barrier feeders, payment-machine supplies, ventilation interface.

Distinct requirement: Add three individually labelled external permissives with diagnostic test points.

Functional acceptance: Open each permissive and verify the specified inhibit and reset behaviour.
