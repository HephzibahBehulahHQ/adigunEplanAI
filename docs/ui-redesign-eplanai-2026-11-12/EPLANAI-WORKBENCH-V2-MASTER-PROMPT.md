### Compact command surface
Use a single consistent toolbar language: e.g., **16–18px** visible icons on approximately **30–36px** buttons, identical padding, alignment, hit targets and hover/focus states. The persistent compact toolbar is **icon-first with accessible label/tooltips**, without mixing giant decorative symbols and tiny controls. When opened, menu flyout commands display **16px icons + 12–13px text labels**, grouped, no icon-only mystery actions. Exact values are design goals subject to text zoom and touch a11y. Respect 44×44px touch targets when applicable, via spacing/mobile adaptation.

### All transient menu rules
- Click a trigger opens its anchored flyout; click same closes; click another switches one active popover without layout shift.
- Only one transient popover/menu per menu manager. Mouse/pen pointer exit the combined trigger **and panel** closes after ~250–350ms grace; entering the panel cancels the timer. Do NOT close due to small gap/flicker, content scroll, active drag, focus inside or menu switching.
- Touch tap toggles; outside tap closes; never depend on hover for touch. Press Escape closes and restores focus to trigger; outside click closes without altering selection.
- Use stable **fixed-position/portal** or positioned overlay and viewport-aware anchor measurement; flip/clamp near edges; max-height `calc(100dvh - chrome - safe-area)`, vertical scrolling **inside flyout only**. Handle zoom, RTL-like long translations, resize, scroll, and focus trapping only for actual modal dialogs.
- `HomeRibbon` modal workflows for placing, catalog, validation, symbol editing and active forms remain genuine modal workflows where appropriate, **not mouseleave-closing transient panels**. BOT docked/floating is persistent; never auto-dismiss it. File picker / save dialog / edits are also persistent.
- Open/close/switch transient menu does NOT change bounding rectangles of `.workbench-header`, `.workbench-toolbar`, `.engineering-layout`, `.workspace`, `.drawing-sheet`, `.status-bar`, or native canvas. Layout change tolerance <=1 CSS px in tests.
- Maintain proper `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`, keyboard Tab/arrow/Enter/Escape, focus visible, and correct screen-reader descriptions. Avoid inaccessible focusable children inside inert/hidden flyouts; dialogs/modal focus independently.
