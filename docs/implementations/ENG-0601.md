## Required behaviour

Local start and stop, pressure permissive, visible operating indication and
lockable maintenance isolation. Loss and restoration of power must not cause
uncommanded restart. Ordinary control interlocks are not safety functions.
PE is continuous and never switched. Panel and field equipment remain distinct.

## Source research started

### Pressure permissive candidate — primary document inspected 2026-11-15

Telemecanique Sensors XMLB004A2S11, manufacturer datasheet dated 2024-07-30:
https://downloads.telemecaniquesensors.com/dam/datasheets/en/XMLB004A2S11_EN.pdf
The document specifies fresh-water service, G1/4 female connection, one
changeover contact, adjustable rising/falling thresholds and IP66 enclosure.
Its 158 × 35 × 77.5 mm body dimensions are distinct from packaging dimensions.
Electrical terminal mapping is a diagram on page 5 and remains unverified;
do not invent contact numbers or add a supposedly connectable verified part yet.
The published DC-13 value is at 250 V, not a verified 24 V utilization rating.
Pressure thresholds are design settings requiring hydraulic justification,
not manufacturer-prescribed pump operating points.

Primary source candidate: Siemens 3RT2015-1BB41:
https://support.industry.siemens.com/teddatasheet/?caller=SIOS&format=pdf&language=en&mlfbs=3RT2015-1BB41
Official description identifies 7 A AC-3, 3 kW at 400 V, 24 V DC coil and one NO
auxiliary contact. Not selected yet: match motor current, overload range,
short-circuit coordination and indication needs before adding it to this design.
Existing 3RT2026 / 3RU2126 / DRN132M4 entries are another candidate combination;
do not combine the 11–16 A overload with the existing 0.405 A DRN63MS4 motor.
